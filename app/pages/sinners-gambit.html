Pass and play

One phone around the table

In November 2026 I started the mobile game over, and the current version uses the board game's rules as its base. The current build is a flat blockout for 2 to 4 players sharing one iPhone. On your turn you roll, then trade, buy dice and use your Sin's powers before you pass the phone. Solo play against bot Sins and the option of one phone per player are planned.

  1. RollYour roll scores

    A 4, 5 or 6 scores 40, 50 or 60. Then you buy, trade or use a power before you pass.

  2. DealGifts and promises

    You can give points, lend a die or buy another player a die. The other player taps to agree. A promise made out loud binds no one.

  3. LustWhisper of Desire

    Before scoring, Lust swaps one of its dice for a higher die from another player's last roll.

  4. WrathVengeful Strike

    The target chooses: lose 50 points, or give Wrath a die for two full rounds.

  5. PurgatoryOut of play

    Roll nothing above a 3 and you go to Purgatory. You can't buy, trade or use powers until a later roll shows two or more dice from 1 to 3.

Blockout

Four rounds of the table

The first mobile version was a race to 1,000 against up to six rivals, like the board game. I blocked out the table in flat shapes and a five-color palette, with only the stained glass and the dice as art, and reviewed each round at three phone sizes with four and seven players.

  1. Round 1The manuscript table

    The score track runs down the margin like notes in a manuscript.

  2. Round 2A wide track

    The track moves across the top, the candles get large and the active window doubles in size.

  3. Round 3True scores

    Each player's sigil sits at their true score on a full-width band, and the risk shows in your candle flames.

  4. Round 4Playable

    Small markers on stems, points over the dice and costs shown on what they hit. This round was the first playable table.

Where it started

The board game

Sinners' Gambit began in 2016 as a tabletop game I made with Theresa Devine. I redesigned it on my own in 2025 and published it on The Game Crafter.

A race to 1,000

Players score 40, 50 or 60 for each 4, 5 or 6 they roll, and the first to 1,000 points escapes.

Purgatory

Roll nothing above a 3 and you go to purgatory. You climb out by rolling two or more dice from 1 to 3.

The Gambit Phase

Between rolls, players negotiate, trade, buy dice for 20 points and use their Sin's powers.

Seven Sins

Each Sin has two powers: one free and one that unlocks at 350 points.
