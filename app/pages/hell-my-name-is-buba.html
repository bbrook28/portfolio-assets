Epic MegaJam 2021 · Theme: "Running out of space"

Hell, My Name
Is Buba

Hell's lone janitor holds the Hellfire Arena with a blunderbuss. Every shell he fires stays on the floor, so the arena literally runs out of space.

Role
Game Design · Programming · SFX
Team
5 developers
Engine
Unreal Engine 4
Format
Game jam

Reading the theme

Your ammo becomes the level.

Spent shells turn into permanent, collidable geometry. An open floor slowly becomes a cluttered battlefield shaped by how you played, and every shot you take costs future movement.

I programmed the persistent-shell system so it stayed performant as hundreds of casings piled up, and owned the SFX logic that gives each shell its weight and clink. Enemies were designed to use the clutter against Buba, turning his own discarded weapons on him.

The arsenal

Default

Hellfire Shells

Infinite. Two shots per base enemy. Discarded shells can explode when touched.

Fire

Satan's Breath

Massive fireballs that one-shot weak enemies with area damage on impact.

Utility

Sulfur Shot

A gas burst that launches enemies and debris and can push Buba back mid-air.

Sustain

Angel's Tears

An area field that heals 25–35% of health while you stand in it.
