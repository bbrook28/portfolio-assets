Reading the theme

Your ammo becomes the level.

Instead of a timer or a shrinking room, we made the player the cause. Spent shells turn into permanent, collidable geometry, so an open floor slowly becomes a cluttered battlefield shaped by how you played.

I programmed the persistent-shell system so it stayed performant as hundreds of casings piled up, and owned the SFX logic that gives each shell its weight and clink. The result is a siege: hold out as long as you can, and spend every shot knowing it costs you room to move.

Systems design

The life of a shell.

One object does four jobs: it's your ammo, then your cover, then a trap, then the reason you lose.

  1. Fire

    Buba's single-round shotgun reloads after every shot, so each one is a decision.

  2. Settle

    The spent casing drops and stays, becoming solid geometry with real collision.

  3. Cover

    Aggressive play builds more cover to hide behind, a reward for risk.

  4. Trap

    Discarded shells can explode when touched, for Buba and for the enemies trying to turn his weapons on him.

  5. Crowd

    Eventually the floor fills. The run ends when you've boxed yourself in.

Interactive · Hold the arena

Every shot is space you won't get back.

Click or tap to fire toward that point. Each shot leaves a shell behind. The damned walk around your shells, and any shell they touch goes off. Watch the space meter.

The arsenal

Default

Hellfire Shells

Infinite. Two shots per base enemy. Discarded shells can explode when touched.

Fire

Satan's Breath

Massive fireballs that one-shot weak enemies with area damage on impact.

Utility

Sulfur Shot

A gas burst that launches enemies and debris and can push Buba back mid-air.

Sustain

Angel's Tears

An area field that heals 25–35% of health while you stand in it.

The damned

Enemies work together. The lost and the damned try to shoot their way to freedom, while others go after Buba's own discarded shells to use them against him, so the clutter you create is never only yours.
