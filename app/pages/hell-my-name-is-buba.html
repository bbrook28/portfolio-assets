Reading the theme

Spent shells become the level

Instead of a timer or a shrinking room, we made the player the cause. Spent shells turn into permanent, solid geometry, so an open floor slowly fills up based on how you played. I programmed the shell system so it stayed fast with hundreds of casings on the floor, and owned the SFX that give each shell its clink.

  1. FireEvery shot counts

    The single-round shotgun reloads after every shot.

  2. SettleThe shell stays

    The casing drops and becomes solid geometry.

  3. CoverRisk builds walls

    Aggressive play builds more cover to hide behind.

  4. TrapShells explode

    Touched shells can blow up, for Buba and for enemies.

  5. CrowdYou box yourself in

    The run ends when the floor is full.

The game

In the arena

Hellfire Shells

The default ammo, and it never runs out. Basic enemies take two shots, so most of the clutter comes from these.

Satan's Breath

Fireballs that one-shot weak enemies with area damage.

Sulfur Shot

A gas burst that launches enemies and debris, and can push Buba back mid-air.

Angel's Tears

A field that heals 25 to 35% of health while you stand in it.

The damned

Some enemies shoot their way to freedom. Others pick up Buba's shells to use against him.
