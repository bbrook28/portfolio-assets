The brief

"Treat the courthouse like a dungeon"

I owned the hub of the vertical slice: a multi-story courthouse built as a "Swiss cheese" stronghold with many ways in, connected underground to a police station and old jail. The project was canceled so the studio could focus on other IP after the vertical slice was complete, with the hub fully playable.

I worked from the narrative team's room-by-room brief, where the lobby, courtroom, and offices each carried a piece of the town's story. My job was to turn that into a layout that supports stealth, combat, and exploration in VR.

Interactive · Paper to path

From room layout to player route

Every space started as a top-down paper map. The overview fixes the rooms and doors. The gameplay pass draws the intended route, red X's for blocked paths, walkers, loot, and hidden crawl-throughs. Drag to compare.

Overview Gameplay route

Courthouse

Three floors and a basement. The critical path snakes through the first floor, climbs to the courtroom and conference rooms, then drops into the basement and out through the tunnel. Doors marked with an X force players to find another way: up through holes in the ceiling, across the second floor, down a ladder.

Police station

A compact building of cells, interrogation, a firing range, and the sheriff's gun storage. Walkers, cabinets, and desks are placed to break sightlines. Hidden crouch-holes between the cells give stealthy players a way past.

Iteration

Three rounds of lead feedback

Each review produced concrete changes: tightening the micro layout, sizing spaces for VR bodies, and making vertical routes essential.

Round 1 · Feb 2

Sized for VR bodies: 10×10 jail cells, a smaller jury room, a split-up second floor, and a tunnel wide enough for two with a 45° turn at the end to block sightlines.

Round 2 · Feb 5

"Treat the courthouse like a dungeon." The focus moved to the micro layout, with pipes, holes, and ladders as alternate routes.

Round 3 · Mar 12

Infested the mechanics bay and put an essential item upstairs, so more players take the vertical route. The station footprint grew where the layout needed it.

Permeability

"Swiss cheese" layout

Sewers, roof, windows, and broken walls: more than a dozen ways in, so stealth and aggressive players each find their own approach.

Gating

Locks you can see

Broken stairwells, flooded basements, and barricaded doors are diegetic locks. Keys and upgrades reopen wings players already passed, like a Metroidvania.

Connection

One continuous world

The tunnel joins two distinct biomes, a civic courthouse and an industrial police compound, with sightlines and streaming set up so the walk between them has no break.
