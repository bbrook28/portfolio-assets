The brief

Treat the courthouse like a dungeon.

I owned the hub of the vertical slice: a multi-story courthouse built as a "Swiss cheese" stronghold with many ways in, connected underground to a police station and old jail. The project was canceled to prioritize other IP after the vertical slice was complete, with the hub fully playable.

I worked from the narrative team's room-by-room brief, where the lobby, courtroom, and offices each carried a piece of the town's story. My job was to turn that into a layout that supports stealth, combat, and exploration in VR.

Interactive · Paper to path

The layout, then the route through it.

Every space started as a top-down paper map. The overview fixes the rooms and doors. The gameplay pass draws the intended route, red X's for blocked paths, walkers, loot, and hidden crawl-throughs. Drag to compare.

Overview Gameplay route

Courthouse

Three floors and a basement. The critical path snakes through the first floor, climbs to the courtroom and conference rooms, then drops into the basement and out through the tunnel. Doors marked with an X force players to find another way: up through holes in the ceiling, across the second floor, down a ladder.

Police station

A compact building of cells, interrogation, a firing range, and the sheriff's gun storage. Walkers, cabinets, and desks are placed to break sightlines. Hidden crouch-holes between the cells give stealthy players a way past.

Iteration

Three rounds of lead feedback.

Each review produced concrete changes: tightening the micro layout, sizing spaces for VR bodies, and making vertical routes essential rather than optional.

  1. Feb 2, 2024
    • Jail cells resized to 10×10
    • Jury room shrunk and Bailiff's office expanded
    • Second-floor layout broken up into smaller spaces
    • Tunnel widened to fit two people side by side
    • Added a 45° upward turn at the tunnel's end to block sightlines
  2. Feb 5, 2024
    • Shifted focus to the micro layout
    • Lead note: “treat the courthouse like a dungeon,” which became the layout's guiding rule
    • Added alternative traversal: pipes, holes, ladders
  3. Mar 12, 2024
    • Infested the mechanics bay so the upper pathway becomes essential
    • Placed an essential item up there, so more players experience the verticality
    • Station footprint enlarged where the layout needed it
Permeability

"Swiss cheese" layout

Sewers, roof, windows, and broken walls: more than a dozen ways in, so stealth and aggressive players each find their own approach.

Gating

Show, don't tell

Broken stairwells, flooded basements, and barricaded doors are diegetic locks. Keys and upgrades reopen wings players already passed, like a Metroidvania.

Connection

One continuous world

The tunnel joins two distinct biomes, a civic courthouse and an industrial police compound, with sightlines and streaming managed so it never breaks immersion.
