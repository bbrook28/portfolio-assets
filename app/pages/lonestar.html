Project: Lonestar

The central hub of a canceled Walking Dead VR spinoff: a small-town courthouse, a police station and the tunnel between them, taken from paper maps to a playable Unreal Engine 5 slice.

The brief

"Treat the courthouse like a dungeon"

I worked from the narrative team's room-by-room brief, where the lobby, courtroom and offices each carried a piece of the town's story. My job was a layout that supports stealth, combat and exploration in VR. The studio canceled the project to focus on other IP once the slice was done.

Swiss cheese

Sewers, roof, windows and broken walls: more than a dozen ways in, so stealthy and aggressive players each find an approach.

Locks you can see

Broken stairwells, flooded basements and barricaded doors block the way. Keys and upgrades reopen wings players already passed.

One world

The tunnel joins a civic courthouse and an industrial police compound with no break in the walk.

Sized for VR

The jail cells are 10 by 10, the jury room got smaller, and the tunnel fits two people, with a 45° turn that blocks sightlines.

Paper to path

Room layout to player route

Every space started as a top-down paper map. The overview fixes rooms and doors. The gameplay pass draws the route, red X's for blocked paths, walkers, loot and hidden crawl-throughs. Drag to compare.
