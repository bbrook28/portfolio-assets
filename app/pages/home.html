How I work

Spaces that explain themselves

I want players to know where they are without a map. In Behemoth's castle hub, a central arch reads from every corner of the courtyard, so you always have your bearings.

Layout first, then the route

Almost everything I make starts as a top-down layout. Then I draw the route on it: where players go, where the walkers wait, and where the loot and hidden crawl-throughs are. This is the police station from Lonestar.
