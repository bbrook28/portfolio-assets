What I owned
Locations and quests for the first two regions: missions from paper design and blockout through Alpha, critical-path objectives, AI combat encounters and layouts, and the Blueprint scripting for them.
My part

A world that reacts

The goal

Locations and quests where the world changes based on what the player does, and keeps changing after they leave.

What I did

I took missions from paper design and blockout through Alpha, built their encounters, and scripted the interaction chains in Blueprints myself, so fewer requests went to engineering. Most of it is still under NDA.

What I learned

How to tell a story in an immersive sim. Players can miss any part of it, so each quest's story had to bend around what they skip and still hold up on its own.

Missions

I took core narrative missions from paper design and blockout through Alpha, including their pacing and combat beats.

Encounters

I built critical-path objectives, AI combat encounters and their layouts to the creative director's vision.

Scripting

I scripted gameplay moments and interaction chains in Blueprints.

Polish

I led iteration and late polish on our content with Art, Audio and Narrative.
