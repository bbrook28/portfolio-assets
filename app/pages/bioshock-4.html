Directive

Building a world that reacts

On the Level Design team, I worked on the first two regions of the game. I designed locations and quests where the world changes based on what the player does, and keeps changing when they leave.

Most specifics are under NDA. Below is one side quest, with names left out, that shows how it worked.

01

End-to-end mission ownership

Core narrative missions from paper design and blockout through Alpha, defining critical-path pacing and combat beats in Unreal Engine 5.

02

Content direction

Critical-path objectives, dynamic AI combat encounters, and complex layouts, following the creative director's vision.

03

Blueprint ownership

Scripted bespoke gameplay moments and interaction chains myself, reducing engineering overhead and connecting narrative intent to systemic play.

04

Cross-discipline alignment

Led Art, Audio, and Narrative partners through data-informed iteration and late polish to set the quality bar for our content.

Interactive · Side quest

The museum cache

Two rival groups are hunting for a hidden supply cache in an abandoned museum. Players can stumble into it, and what they do there decides what they find when they come back. Play it below by clicking the glowing boxes on the story map.

Names left out and some details filled in for this demo. It's my design from development and may have changed since.

Story map · 0/6 endings found

  • MemoryEach choice changes the state the player returns to, so consequences play out between visits
  • EscalationKilling both groups doesn't end the story; it starts a faction war the player caused
  • CluesNames and dates point to exhibits around the building and lead to a hidden switch that opens a secret door
  • Stealth pathPlayers who decipher the clues can take the stash at any point and let the groups blame each other
  • SecurityTurrets and security systems can be destroyed, disarmed, or hacked. Players can use them to fight or to sneak
  • PowerCutting the power at the fuse box shuts off security and the secret door. Enemies present go to investigate the fuse box: if they catch you it's a fight, but it also lures them away from where you need to go
  • AudioI added a combat audio system where enemies react to their comrades' deaths with voice lines
