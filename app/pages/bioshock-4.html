Directive

A world that keeps living when you look away.

As part of the Living World strike team, I worked on the systemic heartbeat of the next BioShock, evolving the franchise's signature immersive-sim play into a world that exists and reacts independently of the player.

The game is unannounced beyond its existence, so the specifics stay under NDA. Here is what I can share about the work itself.

  1. End-to-end mission ownership

    Core narrative missions from paper design and blockout through Alpha, defining critical-path pacing and combat beats in Unreal Engine 5.

  2. Content direction

    Critical-path objectives, dynamic AI combat encounters and complex layouts, kept aligned with the creative director's vision.

  3. Blueprint ownership

    Scripted bespoke gameplay moments and interaction chains myself, reducing engineering overhead and connecting narrative intent to systemic play.

  4. Cross-discipline alignment

    Led Art, Audio and Narrative partners through data-informed iteration and late polish to set the quality bar for Living World content.

Immersive sim
