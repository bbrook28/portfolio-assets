My part

A world that reacts

I designed locations and quests where the world changes based on what the player does, and keeps changing after they leave. Most of it is under NDA, so below is one side quest with the names left out.

Missions

I took core narrative missions from paper design and blockout through Alpha, including their pacing and combat beats.

Encounters

I built critical-path objectives, AI combat encounters and their layouts to the creative director's vision.

Scripting

I scripted gameplay moments and interaction chains in Blueprints myself, so fewer requests went to engineering.

Polish

I led iteration and late polish on our content with Art, Audio and Narrative.

Side quest

The museum cache

Two rival groups are hunting for a supply cache hidden in an abandoned museum. Players can stumble into it at any point. What they do there decides what they find when they come back.

01

Leave them alone

Walk away and come back later.

They finished each other off

The rival group showed up while you were gone, and the two groups killed each other. The stash is still hidden.

Beaten to it

The rivals cracked the clues. The hidden room is open, and empty.

02

Fight them

Kill the group in the museum, and later the rivals too.

A war you started

Killing both groups doesn't end it. On your third visit both factions retaliate, and you walk into a faction war you caused.

03

Take the stash

Follow the clues to a secret door. The turret and the fuse box are tools along the way.

Thieves fall out

Nobody sees you. Later each group accuses the other of stealing the stash, and they fight.

Caught red-handed

They spot you with the stash, call for backup and try to kill you.

Caught in the open

The group checking the noise or the fuse box finds you, and it turns into a fight. A hacked turret fights on your side.

Names left out and some details filled in. It's my design from development and may have changed since.

The systems behind it

Memory

Each choice changes the state you come back to, so consequences play out between visits.

Clues

Names and dates point to exhibits around the building and lead to a hidden switch for the secret door.

Security

Turrets can be destroyed, disarmed or hacked, and used to fight or to sneak.

Power

Cutting power at the fuse box shuts off security and the secret door. Enemies go to check the fuse box, which can mean a fight, but it also pulls them away from where you need to go.

Audio

I added a combat audio system where enemies react to their comrades' deaths with voice lines.
