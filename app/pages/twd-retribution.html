Game feel

Chainsaw haptics

I designed and tuned every haptic curve on the chainsaw, from the first pull of the ripcord to the sputter of an empty tank. These are the 12 shipped curves, drawn to the same scale: height is intensity, width is duration up to 2 seconds.

Start-up

Ripcord1.5 s

A subtle loop while the cord is being pulled.

Ripcord blip0.5 s

The kick as the motor catches, handing off to Motor on.

Motor on1.6 s

An even pulse that reads as an idling two-stroke engine.

Running

Throttle held1.6 s

Revving hard: stronger, tighter pulses than idle.

Throttle released2 s

Winds down smoothly before returning to Motor on.

Add gas2 s

Refuelling, held while the can pours.

Cutting

Sever meat2 s

Rhythmic, and slightly softer than bone.

Sever bone2 s

Bone hits harder. Playtests slowed it down so each impact lands.

Sever stuck0.5 s

The blade binds in a body: a sharp full-strength jolt.

Bounce off0.5 s

A short burst when the saw glances off armor or bone.

Shut-down

Low fuel1 s

An intense sputter that warns the tank is nearly empty.

Motor off1.6 s

The engine dying out.

Playtests shaped the final feel. Testers asked for the ripcord to build in three beats, like a real engine. Strength was tuned per headset, and testing caught the haptic firing on the wrong hand. Now both hands feel the saw while it runs.

Enemy design

Two new Tower enemies

For players who mastered Chapter 1

Eradicators

The Tower's strongest troops. Every rule pushes the player toward something other than a headshot.

  • Helmets and face coverings make headshots unreliable.
  • One grenade-launcher round kills a human. Walkers take two.
  • They rush the player and only take cover when hurt.
  • Built on the existing enemy system, so they stayed cheap to make.
For players who hide behind cover

Grenadiers

VR players love to hunker down. Grenadiers lob explosives to flush them out and carry .30 revolvers up close.

  • Throw range of 550 to 2,000 units, tunable per spawn.
  • A 6-second fuse. Catch the grenade and you get 2.5 more seconds to throw it back.
  • They won't throw near allies.
  • Grenade type, arc height and fuse are all set in Blueprint.

Systems design

Night Mode loot

Survivors who died during the day leave fresh bodies with backpacks of night-only items. Players who go out after dark find loot on the people who didn't make it. I wrote the spec as a toolset, so designers could tune it without code.

Spawning

Designer-set minimum and maximum bodies per map, with a weighted distribution.

Loot tiers

Separate weighted tables for average and rare finds.

Interaction

Backpacks open with the same UX as the player's own.

Stretch goal

Turn a pack upside down and shake it to empty it.
