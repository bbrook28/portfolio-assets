GMTK Game Jam 2020 · Theme: "Out of control"

Wild Magic

A fantasy bullet-hell where every spell has kickback. Wizzo's out-of-control magic launches him across the arena, so aiming is also moving.

Role
Game Design · VFX · SFX
Team
7 developers
Engine
Unreal Engine
Format
Game jam

Core mechanic

Your strongest spell is also your worst idea.

High-velocity spells push Wizzo backwards. Fire a big one near a hazard and the recoil can kill you. That single rule turns a twin-stick shooter into a game about timing, positioning and restraint.

  • CountersEach enemy type is weak to a different spell, so players must switch
  • RiskKickback scales with spell power: big damage, big displacement
  • ArenaHazards are placed to punish careless recoil
  • FeedbackI owned VFX and SFX so every cast reads and lands
