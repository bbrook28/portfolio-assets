Reading the theme

Your strongest spell is also your worst idea.

"Out of control" usually means the player loses control. We wanted the player to choose how much control to give up. Small spells barely move Wizzo; big ones hit hard and fling him backwards. Every cast is a trade between damage and footing.

  • CountersEach enemy type is weak to a different spell, so players can't just spam the safe one
  • RiskKickback scales with spell power: big damage, big displacement
  • ArenaHazards sit where careless recoil will carry you
  • MovementRecoil can double as repositioning, if you aim it well
  • FeedbackI owned VFX and SFX so every cast reads and lands

Interactive · Try the kickback

Aim, cast, and mind the edges.

A tiny recreation of the core rule. Click or tap to cast toward that point, and pick a spell below. Bigger spells hit harder and throw Wizzo farther back. Land in the fire and you start over.

Controls

Twin-stick, with a dash.

Move on the left stick, aim and fire on the right, and dash on the bumper. The dash is the one fully controlled move in your kit, a way to correct after a big blast throws you somewhere bad. Keyboard and mouse are supported too.

Player feedback

What players said, and what we took away.

The lesson: the theme landed, but "out of control" has to feel deliberate. Players read rough controls and intended chaos the same way, so the post-jam plan centered on impact: more oomph on each spell and its hits, so the recoil reads as the spell's power rather than slippery movement.
