About

Ben Brook

I'm a game designer at Nexon America, on the team making MapleStory Classic World. Most of my time goes into Forgotten Hollow, a new region under Ellinia that opens November 18. I built most of its maps and wrote dozens of its quests.

Résumé (PDF) LinkedIn

Before Nexon I was a level designer at Cloud Chamber on BioShock 4. Before that I was at Skydance for two years, on The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners – Chapter 2: Retribution and then Behemoth.

I like spaces that explain themselves. You can see the landmark from anywhere in the room, and you know what the locked door wants before you find the key. Almost everything I make starts on paper.

Then I build it myself. At Skydance and Cloud Chamber that meant scripting combat, AI and one-off moments in Blueprints. At Nexon I put quests, maps and NPCs straight into the game's data and see them through QA.

Outside work I'm a mentor with Amir Satvat's Always Supporting the Games Community. I help people find their way into games.

How I work

Doc and data agree

If one changes, I update the other.

Check the whole change

When I edit a quest I look at its text, hand-offs, prerequisites, level, EXP and rewards, then the doc.

QA gets a note

Every change goes over with what changed and how to test it.

Boring data, fun content

I follow the project's data conventions to the letter and save the ideas for what players see.
