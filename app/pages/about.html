About

I design the places between the story beats.

I'm a game and level designer working across AAA, live ops, and VR since 2022. I work where art meets engineering: I take high-level creative direction and turn it into spaces, systems, and quests that are concrete and playable.

I'm a technical craftsman first. Strong visual scripting, AI behavior, and combat logic let me prototype complex systems on my own and keep design and code talking to each other. I care most about spaces that tell their own story, systems that give players real choices, and the small details of feel, like a chainsaw's rumble or a jump that forgives a late press.

Today I'm a Game Designer at Nexon America on MapleStory Classic World, where I designed the Forgotten Hollow region.

Outside the studio I'm a mentor with Amir Satvat's Always Supporting the Games Community, helping people find their way into the games industry.
