About

About me

I'm a Game Designer at Nexon America on the Western MapleStory development team, where I lead level and quest design for Global MapleStory Classic World.

Before Nexon, I was a Level Designer at Cloud Chamber, building immersive, player-driven environments for the upcoming BioShock 4. Before that, I worked on The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners – Chapter 2: Retribution and Skydance's Behemoth at Skydance.

My approach to level design has always been about balancing art, gameplay, and narrative to create spaces that feel both believable and fun to play in. I love collaborating across disciplines, from design and art to narrative and systems, to make sure every piece comes together for the player.

I'm also a hands-on designer. I script gameplay, AI, and combat in Blueprints, and at Nexon I build quests, maps, and NPCs directly in the game's data, then take them through QA to release.

Outside the studio I'm a mentor with Amir Satvat's Always Supporting the Games Community, helping people find their way into the games industry.

Approach

How I work

  1. The design doc is the source of truth

    Data has to match it, and I keep the docs in sync with the data.

  2. Check every part of a change

    Text, hand-offs, prerequisites, level and EXP, rewards, and docs.

  3. Clear handoffs to QA

    I write clear handoffs to QA for every change.

  4. Conventional structure, creative content

    I follow the existing conventions in how data is structured and stay creative in the content players see.
