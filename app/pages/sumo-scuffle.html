Origin

Class project to App Store

It started as a four-person SCAD prototype that I designed and programmed. I rebuilt it alone as a commercial release: design, programming, art and audio direction, playtesting, App Store submission and marketing. The goal was a game anyone can pick up at a party.

Couch first

Up to four players on one phone, with a face-to-face layout and a mirrored scoreboard for a phone lying flat on a table. Controllers work too.

Readable chaos

Four players and falling food get noisy fast, so every system keeps the bout readable and the score close.

Solo too

CPU opponents adjust to a hidden skill rating after every bout, so nobody can pick Easy and coast.

Playtesting

Designing for non-gamers

I playtested with people who rarely play games. Each round changed how the game is controlled and taught.

Tilt by default

Testers fought the joystick, so I added tilt controls: tip the phone to roll, tap anywhere to charge. They're now the default.

Flick to charge

One tester steered with a finger they never lifted, so they couldn't charge. I added flick-to-charge and a tip that teaches it.

Play first

New players had to pick controls before they'd moved. Now the Dojo starts them rolling right away and asks afterwards.

Tips step aside

Lesson tips covered what testers were trying to do, so they shrink out of the way once you move.

Reward the aim

A tester mashed the charge button, so missed charges now recharge more slowly.

Let players tune it

A sensitivity slider covers stick, tilt, follow and gamepad.

Modes

Four ways to scuffle

Power-ups

There are six, built on sumo food and festival themes. Each has a clear pickup and a ring that blinks before it wears off.

Catch-up

Whoever is behind gets an aura, the leader wears a crown, and the last seconds are worth double.

Replay

Each bout's best moment plays back in slow motion, and players can share it as a clip.

Seasons

Yokai Night and a Winter Festival, each with its own arena, food and music.

Career

Ten real sumo ranks to climb, with gear on the way up and a champion boss bout at the top.

Readability

Colorblind cues on top of color: star edges on power-up rings and spikes on bad food.
