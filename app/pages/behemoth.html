Opening chapter

The castle hub

Behemoth's levels are big and open, so players can take objectives their own way between boss fights. This hub is where they learn how those spaces work: a central courtyard you can read at a glance, with several routes out.

  1. ArrivalA large entryway

    The gate frames the castle and sets its scale before players step in.

  2. OrientationThe central arch

    A landmark you can see from anywhere in the courtyard, so you always know where you are.

  3. RoutesConnections to two areas

    In the shipped game, routes lead off past the chapel ruins and the tower.

The arch

It anchors the middle of the space and reads from every corner.

Chapel ruins

Climbing routes up the rubble give the hub some height.

Area concept

The fight with the Hollow King

I wrote the concept for the game's final area, where the campaign's last fight happens. It had to pay off hours of buildup.

  • The biggest sense of scale in the game.
  • An arena that reads clearly in VR.
  • A stage that makes the last behemoth feel like the payoff.
