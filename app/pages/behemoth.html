Opening chapter

The castle hub

The problem

Behemoth's levels are big and open, so players can take objectives their own way between boss fights. Early in the game they need a place that teaches how those spaces work.

What I did

I designed the opening chapter's castle hub around a courtyard you can read at a glance: a large entryway that sets the scale, a central arch you can see from anywhere, and routes out to two further areas.

The result

The hub started out much larger and more complex. We found that swinging up vertical spaces with the grappling hook was the most fun thing in it, so I rebuilt the area around the joy and power of that move. It shipped in December 2024, built and lit by the environment art team.

  1. ArrivalA large entryway

    The gate frames the castle and sets its scale before players step in.

  2. OrientationThe central arch

    A landmark you can see from anywhere in the courtyard, so you always know where you are.

  3. RoutesConnections to two areas

    In the shipped game, routes lead off past the chapel ruins and the tower.

Area concept

The fight with the Hollow King

I wrote the concept for the game's final area, where the campaign's last fight happens. It had to pay off hours of buildup.

  • The biggest sense of scale in the game.
  • An arena that reads clearly in VR.
  • A stage that makes the last behemoth feel like the payoff.
