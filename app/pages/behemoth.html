The problem

Behemoth's levels are big and open, so players can take objectives their own way between boss fights. Early in the game they need a place that teaches how those spaces work.

What I did

I designed the opening chapter's castle hub around a courtyard you can read at a glance: a large entryway that sets the scale, a central arch you can see from anywhere, and routes out to two further areas.

The result

The hub started out much larger and more complex. We found that swinging up vertical spaces with the grappling hook was the most fun thing in it, so I rebuilt the area around the joy and power of that move. It shipped in December 2024, built and lit by the environment art team.

The arch

It anchors the middle of the space and reads from every corner.

Chapel ruins

Climbing routes up the rubble give the hub some height.

I also wrote the concept for the game's final area, the arena for the last fight of the campaign.
