Zoélie

A combat-free 3D puzzle platformer about a girl whose imagination remakes her town while she searches for her sister. Built by 39 students in 20 weeks, mostly remotely, during the pandemic.

The pitch

A quilt that comes to life

My role

Lead gameplay designer

Zoélie's older sister Sofia is moving away. Heartbroken, Zoélie lets her imagination run wild: a stray cat becomes her sidekick Chonkers, and her sister's quilt comes to life as Sueño, a companion that changes the world around her.

I owned the core mechanics, the 3Cs (character, camera, control) and puzzle design, working with the level, narrative and systems designers. The game won Best Gameplay at the 2021 Intel University Games Showcase, ahead of USC and SMU Guildhall, and was released free. A year earlier it was a Game of the Year finalist at The Rookies 2020, where it was also Highly Commended and a Draft Selection.

Core mechanic

Sueño's forms

With no combat, every obstacle had to be solved through movement and imagination. We prototyped a long list of quilt abilities and cut it down to the ones that each change how Zoélie moves.

Parachute and glider

Float down from rooftops, or glide across the plaza.

Bridge

Stretched taut, the quilt spans gaps too wide to jump.

Sail

On the beach level Zoélie rides giant sea turtles like a windsurfer.

Imagination

Wherever Sueño flies, buildings glow with graffiti and hidden maps appear.

Rope

Dropped from above, Sueño becomes a rope for climbing walls.

Trampoline

Blended into the world, the quilt launches Zoélie high into the air.

Distraction

Zoélie gets past threats with wit: Sueño knocks over trash cans to distract foes and scares off stray cats and birds.

Puzzle design

Two levels

Level 1: the graffiti fish

Zoélie needs a fancy fish to win Chonkers over. A graffiti fish comes to life and swims away, even through the air, from hiding spot to hiding spot. Each spot pulls the player past the plaza's best views and teaches a traversal skill, ending at the fountain.

Level 2: three islands

The King Fiddler Crab takes Chonkers hostage. To free him, Zoélie needs three shells from three islands, each a different kind of test.

  1. RaceThe turtle island

    Windsurf a sea turtle through buoys before they stop blinking, with sharks closing in.

  2. ExploreTreasure Island

    Sueño reveals a hidden map that leads to the top of a stone arch and a second map.

  3. ClimbThe volcano

    The game ends at the summit of an active volcano.
