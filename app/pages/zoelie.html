Zoélie

A combat-free 3D puzzle platformer about a girl whose imagination remakes her town while she searches for her sister. Built by 39 students in 20 weeks, mostly remotely, during the pandemic.

The pitch

The farther from home, the wilder the world.

Zoélie's older sister Sofia is moving away. Heartbroken, Zoélie lets her imagination run wild: a stray cat becomes her talking sidekick Chonkers, and her sister's quilt comes to life as Sueño, a magic companion that transforms the world around her.

The game started as a class-wide pitch competition with one prompt: a family-friendly game about an important human issue. The team set it in a world inspired by Buenos Aires and built it around the bond between siblings. As Lead Gameplay Designer I owned the core mechanics, the 3Cs (character, camera, control), and puzzle design, working alongside the level, narrative, and systems designers.

Best Gameplay, 1st place at the 2021 Intel University Games Showcase, ahead of USC and SMU Guildhall. Released free through the SCAD Games Studio initiative.

Interactive · Core mechanic

One quilt, every tool.

With no combat, every challenge had to be solved through movement and imagination. We prototyped a long list of quilt abilities and narrowed it to a set that each changes how Zoélie moves through space. Choose a form.

Puzzle design · Level 1

Chasing a graffiti fish.

Zoélie needs a fancy fish to win Chonkers over. One graffiti fish comes to life when Sueño passes, then swims away, even through the air, from hiding spot to hiding spot.

The chase is the level's guided tour. Each hiding spot was placed to pull the player past the plaza's best views and teach a traversal skill, ending at the fountain where Zoélie finally grabs the fish.

Puzzle design · Level 2

Three islands, three shells.

Chonkers is taken hostage by the King Fiddler Crab. To free him, Zoélie needs three shells from three islands, each with its own kind of challenge:

  • RaceWindsurf a sea turtle with Sueño as the sail, hitting buoys before they stop blinking while sharks close in
  • ExploreOn Treasure Island, Sueño reveals a hidden map that leads to the top of a stone arch and a second map
  • ClimbThe journey ends at the summit of an active volcano
Game feel

Forgiving, never floaty

Jump arcs, coyote time, and input buffering tuned for a wide audience, with greybox levels built strictly to the character's metrics.

Movement set

More than jumping

Crawling under obstacles, short hops, and a sneak that lets Zoélie eavesdrop on townsfolk. Some conversations hide hints for upcoming puzzles.

Production

Shipped remotely

When COVID-19 hit mid-project, the team kept two-week sprints and daily scrums going over Perforce and Discord across several time zones.
