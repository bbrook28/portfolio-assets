My part

What I built

After the initial concept, building the region was my job. I put it straight into the game's data and took it through QA.

Maps

I built or reworked most of the region's maps. Several changed after playtests.

Quests

I wrote dozens of quests across several story chains, with rewards every class can use.

Monsters

I decided where monsters spawn: safe camps by quest givers, the strongest ones far from the entrances.

NPCs and items

I added new NPCs with dialogue and idle chatter, plus quest items and housing decor.

Quest design

Three chains, one region

One short act pulls players in. Then three chains open at once, gated by level instead of order, and their objectives tour the maps. Finishing the quests means you've seen the place.

Encounter design

A boss with no marker

Field boss · Lv 60

Rotten Mushmom

  • An undead Mushmom, 20 levels above the Victoria Island original.
  • She only spawns in Someone Else's Grave, a hidden map off Decayed Tunnel I. No quest points to her.
  • She regenerates and is weak to holy and fire, the same classes the rest of the Deep Passage favors. That makes her a party fight.

Citizenship quests

Dozens of quests where players earn a home in Henesys and Kerning City. I set the EXP, mesos, item rewards and level requirements.

EXP rebalance

Before launch I retuned quest EXP and rewards across Maple Island and Victoria Island to fit a new leveling curve.

Launch fixes

I fixed a quest reward you could duplicate by re-accepting the quest, and a 1-pixel collision gap that let mages teleport out of a map.
