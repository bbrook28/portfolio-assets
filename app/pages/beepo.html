GMTK Game Jam 2021 · Theme: "Joined together"

Beepo & Pogo

A co-op puzzle game about two robots with magnets on their heads. Line them up, then pull together or push apart to get past each obstacle.

Role
Game Design · VFX · UI
Team
8 developers
Engine
Unreal Engine 4
Mode
Single-player or co-op

Puzzle language

Navigation is the puzzle.

Attraction pulls the pair across gaps. Repulsion launches one past an obstacle the other can't cross. Each of the five puzzles introduces a polarity idea on its own, then combines it with the ones before.

  • ControlsWASD and arrows move each robot, Space toggles the magnets
  • ReadabilityI designed the VFX and UI that make invisible magnetic force visible
  • PlayersSolo players drive both robots; pairs split them

Gameplay capture.

Level layouts

Five puzzles, top down.

Built on a tile grid so every gap and wall is an exact number of magnet lengths.
