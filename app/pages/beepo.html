GMTK Game Jam 2021 · Theme: "Joined together"

Beepo & Pogo

A co-op puzzle game about two robots with magnets on their heads. Line them up, then pull together or push apart to get past each obstacle.

Day one

Why magnets

These were the team's real brainstorm notes for "Joined together." Magnetism won because it turns the theme into something the player does every second: the two robots are bound by a force you switch on and off.

  • Magnetism
  • Welding objects
  • Attracting and repelling
  • Sockets and plugs
  • Soldering
  • Cloned movement
  • Hyperlinks
  • Potions and alchemy
  • Necromancy
  • Linked-room dungeon crawler
  • Trains
  • Sandwich game
My part

Making magnetism readable

Magnetism is invisible, so my VFX and UI work was about readability: when the magnets are live, which way the force acts, and whether the pair is lined up close enough to engage. Audio did the same job, so each sound below maps to a state.

Attract

An electric hum, like a lightning mower.

Repel

A lightning whoosh.

Collide

A hard metal clink.

Win

A fanfare when the stage is complete.

Level layouts

Five puzzles, one tile kit

Every level uses the same floor, holes, buttons and powered walls, so new ideas come from how the pieces combine.

Post-jam

What I took from it

Players liked the idea and the puzzles, and their notes gave me a short list for next time.

Teach the reset

The reset key was there, but players didn't know about it. It belongs in the tutorial.

Check both robots

A co-op exit should only count when both robots reach it.

Tame the physics

Limiting magnet range and pushing objects out of walls would keep the puzzles smooth.
