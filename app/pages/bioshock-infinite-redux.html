BioShock Infinite:
Elizabeth, reimagined

Two design pitches that turn Elizabeth from a companion who tosses supplies into an active partner, and a level design document that teaches one of them in a single room.

The problem

In combat, Elizabeth mostly hands out supplies

Elizabeth is BioShock Infinite's emotional core, yet in a fight she mostly throws the player ammo and health. That gap between story and play is the problem both pitches attack, from two directions.

Pitch 01 · The Tether Initiative

Turning their bond into a combat mechanic

A systemic mechanic that ties Elizabeth's emotional state to the player's tactical choices.

Bond

Tether Gauge

Builds through synchronized actions and unlocks "Anchor" abilities and duo finishers. A low Tether limits her support.

Presence

Active participation

She calls out flankers, suppresses enemies with tear-based debris, and reacts to the player taking damage.

Awareness

Reads the room

She whispers and stays low in stealth and shouts targets in chaos, so her behavior always matches the pacing.

Economy

Skill over luck

Replaces the random supply toss with a predictable economy that rewards aggressive play. Tears become tools she can reposition on command.

Pitch 02 · Design test

A Vigor that Elizabeth draws on

Elizabeth sketches and paints throughout Monument Tower. This Vigor turns that into gameplay. Booker fires paper. Elizabeth draws on it, and what she draws becomes real.

  • Paper CutLight attack: a burst of paper that knocks back and causes bleed
  • Paper TrailHeavy attack: lays a scroll that Elizabeth draws a trap on
  • ContextHer traps match the room's theme: Tesla coils in American history, spike pits in the Hall of Heroes
  • TraversalShe can draw doorways and holes into marked walls and floors
  • CharacterTrap books unlock new drawings. Her aversion to killing can surface as non-lethal traps

Level design document

A one-room level that teaches it

A vandalized Columbia art gallery, drawn to scale on a one-foot grid. Each numbered beat introduces one part of the Vigor, and after the player picks it up, beats 4–6 can be done in any order.
